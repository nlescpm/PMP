%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\subsection{Scope}
\label{sec:scope:sc}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

This document is the official project management protocol of the Netherlands eScience Center. It describes the
activities, responsibilities, and procedures required to manage an eScience project after it has been formally awarded, 
through to its formal closure.

The protocol primarily applies to projects awarded through the eScience Center calls for proposals, although it also 
provides guidance for other types of projects as defined in Section~\ref{sec:scope:types}. It follows the project life
cycle and, for each phase, specifies the required activities, optional activities, responsible roles, and expected
outcomes.

The call process itself is governed by a separate protocol~\cite{call-protocol-2015} and is therefore outside the scope 
of this document. This protocol starts once a project has been formally awarded and covers all activities until the 
project is formally closed.

The following topics are also outside the scope of this protocol:
\begin{itemize}
    \item independent project evaluation (impact, outputs, process, and collaboration);
    \item detailed guidance on project management tools (e.g. \href{https://www.ganttic.com/}{Ganttic}, 
    \href{https://www.metabase.com/}{Metabase dashboards}, \href{https://www.afas.nl/}{AFAS});
    \item general project management methodologies and techniques.
\end{itemize}

The Management Team (MT) approves this protocol and reviews it annually. The document is organised according to the 
project life cycle (Section~\ref{sec:scope:lifecycle}), presenting activities in chronological order.

\subsection{Types of projects}
\label{sec:scope:types}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The eScience Center receives an annual budget from \href{https://www.nwo.nl/}{NWO} and \href{https://www.surf.nl/}{SURF}
, the larger part of which is allocated to projects submitted by researchers working at eligible research performing 
organizations in the Netherlands in the form of the in-kind provision of Research Software Engineers (RSEs). The 
eScience Center also participates in externally funded projects and carries out internally funded strategic initiatives.

These projects differ in their funding model and governance. Throughout this protocol, they are grouped into the 
categories shown in Table~\ref{tab:scope:types}.

At the eScience Center, a standard project lifecycle consists of three phases (see Figure~\ref{fig:project-lifecycle})
initiated, followed by the execution phase, during which research software is developed and project activities are
coordinated and monitored. Finally, the project is formally closed through a structured closing process.

The following sections describe each phase of the project lifecycle in detail.
